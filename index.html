Fat loss habits

  • 10,000 steps every day, no excuses. Rest day, busy day, tired day, it still counts. Make it a habit, not just something that happens on workout days.
  • Keep running as already planned.
  • Calorie deficit and consistency matter more than turning every workout into a cardio circuit.

How to progress

Week 4
Go up a little in dumbbell weight and drop back to 10 reps on the last set

If his form starts to slip, the weight stays where it is.

Intensity and technique

  • Most sets should end with about 1 to 3 good reps still in the tank. No need to hit failure on every set.
  • Quality beats speed. Keep it slow and controlled, especially on squats, lunges, Romanian deadlifts, rows and shoulder presses.
